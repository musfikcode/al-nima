Neurology Services

Our neurology department specializes in diagnosing and treating disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system. We offer advanced neurological care with cutting-edge technology and experienced neurologists.

Our Services Include:

  • Stroke care and prevention
  • Epilepsy management
  • Movement disorders (Parkinson's, tremors)
  • Headache and migraine treatment
  • Neuromuscular disorders
