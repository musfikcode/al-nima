Liver Transplant & GI Surgery

Overview

Liver transplantation is a life-saving surgical procedure that replaces a diseased or failing liver with a healthy one from a deceased or living donor. India is internationally recognized for its advanced transplant programs, offering highly skilled surgeons, cutting-edge facilities, and affordable care compared to Western countries. At Al-Nima, we partner with top-tier hospitals in India to provide comprehensive support for patients requiring this critical procedure.

Types of Liver Transplants

  • Living Donor Liver Transplant: A healthy relative donates a portion of their liver, which regenerates in both the donor and recipient within a few weeks.
  • Deceased Donor Liver Transplant: The entire liver from an individual who has been declared brain-dead is transplanted into the recipient.
  • Split Liver Transplant: A deceased donor’s liver is divided between two recipients, usually an adult and a child.

Why Choose India for Liver Transplants?

  • World-Class Expertise: Indian transplant surgeons are among the most experienced globally, with exceptionally high success rates.
  • State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: Partner hospitals feature dedicated transplant ICUs, advanced imaging technology, and specialized robotic surgery systems.
  • Cost-Effectiveness: Treatment in India costs significantly less than in the US or Europe, without compromising medical quality.
  • Post-Transplant Care: Comprehensive follow-up care and rehabilitation programs ensure long-term health and successful recovery.

How Al-Nima Assists You

We handle all medical visas, hospital admissions, documentation, and coordination between you and the surgical team. Our goal is to make your treatment journey stress-free and seamless.
